# v1.1.0 figure captions

All figures in this directory are generated by `analysis/r7_make_figures.py` from the checksum-bound R6 outputs. No outcome-dependent discrimination filter is used.

## fig1_energy_vs_hopcount.svg
**LEO total-energy effect versus hopcount in the primary static non-interference strata.** Points show the equal-cell weighted percentage effect `LEO - hopcount`; error bars are 95% hierarchical-bootstrap confidence intervals. Negative values indicate lower energy under LEO. Only relayCap=1 has a Holm-significant primary energy reduction.

## fig2_energy_vs_lqi.svg
**LEO total-energy effect versus operational LQI in the primary static non-interference strata.** LEO energy is higher at all three relay caps; all three effects are Holm-significant.

## fig3_availability_vs_hopcount.svg
**LEO adverse availability outcomes versus hopcount.** Ping timeouts and no-route counts are lower under LEO at all three non-interference relay caps, with Holm-significant primary effects.

## fig4_route_divergence.svg
**Direct route divergence from LEO.** Percentage of jointly successful PINGs whose `routeFingerprint` differs between LEO and the comparator, summarized by equal-cell weighting. This is direct route evidence, not an inference from energy equality.

## fig5_nrf_energy_ratio.svg
**Effect of nRF52840 current-table energy accounting on absolute energy scale.** Ratios are nRF-model energy divided by the simplified reconstruction proxy. R6 separately verified zero routing/status/fingerprint/hop mismatches in this sensitivity set.

## fig6_ofat_energy_sensitivity.svg
**One-factor-at-a-time reconstruction-parameter sensitivity.** Bars show total-energy change relative to the frozen LEO reference fixture. This figure is descriptive sensitivity analysis and is not a primary comparative inference.

## fig7_mobility_energy_exploratory.svg
**Exploratory mobility extension.** Total-energy effect of LEO relative to hopcount and LQI under bounded random walk and random waypoint. Mobility is outside the primary source-constrained estimand.

## fig8_interference_energy_limited.svg
**Interference-stratum energy effects.** Each relay-cap/comparator stratum has only five scenario cells. The exact two-sided sign-flip test has a minimum attainable p-value of 0.0625, so these are limited-evidence sensitivity results even when a bootstrap CI excludes zero.
